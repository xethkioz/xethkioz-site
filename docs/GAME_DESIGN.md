# Wildbound campaign blueprint

Core loop: traverse -> survive -> collect -> gear up -> discover companion -> defeat boss/reach gate -> save -> next map.

Biomes: Mossline Meadows, Copper Canyons, Glasswater Ruins, Sunken Mire, Frost Circuit, Neon Hollow, Iron Barrens, The Last Rift.

Boss escalation:
1. Graveljaw: movement pressure and alternating shock shots.
2. Voltwing Prime: jumps plus vertical lightning rain.
3. Mire Matron: minion summons plus corruption shots.
4. Frostwarden: radial zoning patterns.
5. Rift Stalker: teleport repositioning plus spread attacks.
6. Iron Colossus: dense angled volleys and faster phases.
7. The Wild Core: combines previous boss patterns.

Equipment: weapon boosts damage; armor increases durability and reduces damage; charm boosts movement. Gear rarity scales through Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Mythic.

Wildlings unlock on maps 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27 and 31. They follow the player, auto-attack and provide an active pulse skill.
