# WORLD OF XETHKIOZ — Art Bible: Izrdralar v0.1

## Visual thesis
Izrdralar must read as a living Argentine-inspired magical land in 2150: recognizable earth, native vegetation and ruins interrupted by prism magic and temporal fractures. It must not look like generic medieval Europe.

## Golden Region palette
- Deep forest: #1C322D
- Wet foliage: #29493D
- Enchanted water: #244958
- Prism violet: #8B5CF6
- Warm guidance / refuge: #FF8C42
- Bone/light UI: #F0F0F5

## Material language
- Quebracho and ceibo silhouettes for local identity
- Old stone + future-ruin copper
- Prism crystal as the recurring supernatural material
- Weathered textiles, leather and improvised post-collapse technology

## First landmarks
1. Cuenca del Despertar — roots, low ruins, prism scars.
2. First refuge/pueblo — warmth, orange light, human scale.
3. Lago Encantado — turquoise water, safe fauna, Val/Rola/Mela hub.
4. Ruinas vivas — stone/copper/plant integration.
5. Secret cave silhouette — visible early, inaccessible until later gate.

## UI principle
Dark translucent surfaces, restrained violet magic, orange only for guidance/important interactables. The world remains visible behind HUD/dialogue.

## Production rule
Every generated concept must be tagged as reference, approved target, or production-ready asset. Never treat a mood image as a ready sprite/tileset.
